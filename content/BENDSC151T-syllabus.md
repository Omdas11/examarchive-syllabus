Course Objective:  This paper aims to provide instruction on multi-dimensional expansion of 
genres of Bengali literature in twentieth century. It will also discuss how historical events 
shaped Bengali literature in twentieth century. 
 
Course Outcome: Students will be having a historical perspective to understand literature in 
general and Bengali literature in particular. They will be getting basic idea on ideologies of 
modernity vis-a vis its limitations; and shaping of post-colonial thoughts and expressions in the 
context of Bengali literature.
