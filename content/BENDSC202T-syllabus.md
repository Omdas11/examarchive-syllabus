Course Objective:  This paper emphasises on critical reading of nineteenth century Bengali 
prose. Nineteenth century Bengali literature entered a new era of narration in prose, the 
section of literature that directly reveals the tune of contemporary civil space.  
 
Course Outcome: Students will be enriched with creative interpretation of earlier texts and 
social reality and ideological conflicts of contemporary society that are reflected in narratives 
of different genres. Understanding of inter relation of society and literature help in humane 
development of individual.
