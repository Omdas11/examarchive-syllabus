Course Outcome: Students will be capable of reading literature from proper historical perspective. Selected texts would help understanding how life and culture of the toiling mass and socio-political inequality have been represented by contemporary writings; and also how to negotiate earlier texts from rational approach of re reading. This would make the students conscious about different forms of dialectics in given society and encourage them to take a position against the evils.
