Course Objective:  This paper aims to provide general understanding of the genre of Folklore 
and historical development of systematic study of Folklore in Bengali alongside critical 
reading of some forms of folk and oral literature and performance.  
 
Course Outcome: Students will be enriched with knowledge of development of culture as a 
whole; the origin of different expressions, rituals and forms of performance like music and 
drama. Study of this paper would help in developing creativity and critical historical approach 
towards social life and experience.
