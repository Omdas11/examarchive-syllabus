Course Objective:  This paper emphasises on general linguistics, with special reference to 
development of Bengali language and its phonology and morphological aspects.   
 
Course Outcome: Students will be having a clear understanding of the origin and 
development of different language family existent in Indian subcontinent in general and that of 
Bengali language in particular. Phonological and morphological knowledge would help in 
developing writing and understanding skills among the students.
