Course Outcome: Students will enter into the world of literary theories in this paper. Understanding of literary theory is the most important pillar of ability of critical reading of a new text. This would provide every student a tool to become a critique of different fields of human creativity. Knowledge of rhetoric and prosody would give a clear understanding on science of arrangement of words and meanings, for a creative writing.
