DSC-352
Course Title: দেশভাগ ও বাংলা সাহিত্য
Credits: 4 | Contact Hours: 60 | Marks: 100

Course Objective: Partition of India in 1947 jerked the Bengalee society deeply. This paper aims to provide instruction on reflection and depiction of partition and its consequences in Bengali literature.

Course Outcome: Students will be enriched with forms and content of partition literature.
