End Semester Exam Marks: 70;  Internal (CCA) Marks: 30  
 
Course Objective:  Partition of India in 1947 jerked the Bengalee society deeply.   This paper 
aims to provide instruction on reflection and depiction of partition and its consequences in 
Bengali literature. 
Course Outcome: Students will be enriched with forms and content of partition literature. The 
knowledge of Socio-political consequences of partition, the representation of migration and 
diaspora and struggle for rehabilitation in literature will make the students able to understand 
contemporary political and social phenomenon,  and thus help in becoming a responsible 
citizen.
