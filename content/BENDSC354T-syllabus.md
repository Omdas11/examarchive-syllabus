Course Objective: Literature in North east India has some unique features. It is not simply a 
replica of Bengali literature of west Bengal or Bangladesh. The unique societal and political 
reality shapes the tune and temperament of literature of north east India. Through selected 
texts, this course aims to provide the students with the understanding of unique features 
history, culture and polity of North East.       
Course Outcome: Students will be enriched with understanding of the unique features history, 
culture and polity of North East India and literary representation of conflicts, insurgency and 
ethnicity driven identity questions that dominates the political discourse in the region.
