DSM-451
Course Title: বাংলা লোকসাহিত্য ও লোকসংস্কৃতি
Credits: 3 | Contact Hours: 45 | Marks: 100

Course Objective: This paper aims to provide general understanding of the genre of Folklore and historical development of systematic study of Folklore in Bengali alongside critical reading of some forms of folk and oral literature and performance.

Unit-I:
লাকসং ৃ িত : সং া,

Unit-III:
মমনিসংহ গীিতকা (িনবািচত পাঠ- ম য়া, মলু য়া, চ াবতী, দসু কনারােমর পালা)

Unit-V:
বাংলা লাকনাট ছৗ, গ ীরা, গািজ, ওঝা িণিবভাগ, পিরভাষা, ধারাসমূ হ বাদ ও লাককথা

Course Outcome: Students will be enriched with knowledge of development of culture as a whole; the origin of different expressions, rituals and forms of performance like music and drama. Study of this paper would help in developing creativity and critical historical approach towards social life and experience.
