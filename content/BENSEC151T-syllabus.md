Course Objective:  This paper aims to make the learners skilled on DTP and use of ICT in 
creative writing, blogging, academic writing, and content writing in print & electronic media. 
It emphasizes on instruction on MS word and MS Power Point. It also offers instruction on 
proof reading 
 
Course Outcome: Students will be enriched with multi-faced content writing skills, DTP & 
Proof reading; which will make them eligible for getting job in print & electronic media and 
publication sector; and will  also open scope  for self employment, start-up business etc.
