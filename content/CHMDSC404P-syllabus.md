a) To detect the adulterants like paraffin wax/hydrocarbons, dyes and argemone in the fats, oils and ghee.
b) To detect the presence of adulterants like water, proteins, urea, formalin, detergent, sugar and starch in the milk.
c) To detect the adulteration of insoluble substance, chalk powder and washing soda in sugar.
d) To detect the adulteration of Red lead salts/brick powder in chilli powder,
e) To detect the adulteration of yellow lead salts/ colored saw dust in turmeric.
