DSC-251
Course Title: American Literature
Credits: 4 | Contact Hours: - | Marks: 100

Course Objective:
To familiarize students with the different literary forms and terminology found in American literature.
To make them more aware of the topics and literary forms found in American literature.
To expose students, via literary texts, to the socio-cultural context of America
To introduce students to literary works that represent African American, American, and multicultural perspectives in order to improve their comprehension of these themes.
To encourage conversations about American literature and cross-cultural viewpoints

Unit-I: The History and Culture of the United States of America:
1) The Gold Rush and American Dream
2) The American Revolutionary War and the Declaration of Independence
3) The framing of the Constitution, Lincoln and the Civil war
4) Harlem Renaissance

Unit-II: Poetry
1) Ralph Waldo Emerson: "Brahma"
2) Robert Frost: "The Road Not Taken"
3) Emily Dickinson: "Because I could not Stop for Death"
4) Langston Hughes: "Mother to Son"

Unit-III: Novel
1) Ernest Hemingway: The Old Man and the Sea

Unit-IV: Drama
1) Arthur Millar: All My Sons

Unit-V: Short Stories
1) Edgar Allan Poe: "The Purloined letter"
2) Alice Walker: "Everyday Use"

Course Outcome:
The following skills will be acquired by students upon successful completion of the course:
Identify the key historical and cultural changes in American literature.
Review the writings of notable American authors
Examine the different American literary genres, including drama, fiction, and poetry.
Evaluate the relationship between American history and culture and literary literature.
Write a critical essay analyzing the unique literary traits of American literature.

Suggested Readings:
1. Baym, Nina et al. The Norton Anthology of American Literature, Vol. C, D, E Fifth Avenue, USA, 2012.
2. Bloom, Harold. Ed. Short Story Writers and Short Stories. Chelsea House, 2005.
3. David Lehman, John Brehm. Eds. The Oxford Book of American Poetry. OUP, 2006.
4. Sen, Krishana and Ashok Sengupta. A Short History of American Literature, New Delhi: Orient
5. Black Swan, 2017
6. Sheppard, C. A. Ed. A Pageant of Poems. Orient Longman Press. 1998.
7. Voss, Arthur. The American Short Story: A Critical Survey. Univ. of Oklahoma Press, 1980.
