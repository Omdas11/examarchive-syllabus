DSC-351
Course Title: Fundamentals of Literary Theory
Credits: 4 | Contact Hours: - | Marks: 100

Course Objective: This course aims to introduce students to the basics of 20th/ 21st century developments in literary theory in order to encourage them to read and interpret literary texts from particular theoretical perspectives.

Unit-I:
1. Formalism & Structuralism
3. Psychoanalysis

Unit-II:
1. Reader-Response Theory
2. Marxist Theory

Unit-III:
1. Poststructuralism
2. Postmodernism

Unit-IV:
1. Postcolonial Theory
2. New Historicism & Cultural Materialism

Unit-V:
1. Feminist Theory
2. Ecocriticism

Course Outcome: The students will gain knowledge of a variety of theoretical perspectives that are essential to the reading of literature today. They will also learn to apply the same in their own reading of texts.

Suggested Readings:
1. Bertens, Hans. Literary Theory: The Basics. Third Edition. Routledge, 2014.
2. Upstone, Sara. Literary Theory: A Complete Introduction. Teach Yourself, 2017. Recommended Reading: 1 Barry, Peter. Beginning Theory. Viva Books, 2010. 2 Tyson, Lois. Critical Theory Today. Routledge, 2014. .
