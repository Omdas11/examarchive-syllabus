ENGDSC-352 
Postcolonial Literatures 
Course Objectives: This course aims to provide students with a comprehensive exploration of 
postcolonial literatures encompassing various forms and genres. These readings will facilitate 
a critical understanding of the characteristics and significance of postcolonial literatures. 
Learning Outcome: Upon completion of this course, students will be able to critically engage 
with postcolonial literatures using a range of analytical and interpretive skills.
