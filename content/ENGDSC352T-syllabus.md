DSC-352
Course Title: Postcolonial Literatures
Credits: 4 | Contact Hours: - | Marks: 100

Course Objective: This course aims to provide students with a comprehensive exploration of postcolonial literatures encompassing various forms and genres. These readings will facilitate a critical understanding of the characteristics and significance of postcolonial literatures.

Unit-I: Poetry I
1.1 Pablo Neruda: "Tonight I can Write", "The Way Spain Was"
1.2 Derek Walcott: "A Far Cry from Africa", "Names"

Unit-II: Poetry II
2.1 David Malouf: "Revolving Days", "Wild Lemons"
2.2 Mamang Dai: "Small Towns and the River", "The Voice of the Mountain"

Unit-III: Short Story
3.1 Bessie Head: "The Collector of Treasures"
3.2 Ama Ata Aidoo: "The Girl who Can"
3.3 Grace Ogot: "The Green Leaves"

Unit-IV: Fiction I
4.1. Chinua Achebe: Things Fall Apart

Unit-V: Fiction II
5.1 Nilanjan P Choudhury : Shillong Times: A Story of Friendship And Fear

Course Outcome: Upon completion of this course, students will be able to critically engage with postcolonial literatures using a range of analytical and interpretive skills.

Suggested Readings:
1. Bill Ashcroft, Gareth Griffiths,Helen Tiffin (2000): Post-Colonial Studies: The Key Concepts. New York: Routeledge.
2. Leela Gandhi (1998): Postcolonial Theory: A Critical Introduction. Columbia University Press:
3. Franz Fanon (1986): Black Skin, White Masks. Pluto Press, London
