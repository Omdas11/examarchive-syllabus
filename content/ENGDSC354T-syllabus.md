DSC-354
Course Title: Modern European Classics
Credits: 4 | Contact Hours: - | Marks: 100

Course Objective: This course aims to introduce students to a few classics of modern literature written in languages other than English that nevertheless had tremendous influence on literatures all over the world. The course is intended to broaden the student's vision regarding literatures beyond the Anglophone world.

Unit-I: Poetry
1. Charles Baudelaire: "The Carcass"
2. Federico Garcia Lorca: "Lament for Ignacio Sánchez Mejías"
3. Rainer Maria Rilke: "Put Out My Eyes", "What Survives"

Unit-II: Play
1. Bertolt Brecht: The Life of Galileo

Unit-III: Novel I
1. Fyodor Dostoevsky: Crime and Punishment

Unit-IV: Novel II
1. Albert Camus: The Plague

Unit-V: Short Story
1. Anton Chekhov: "The Lady with the Dog"
2. Guy de Maupassant: "The Necklace"

Course Outcome: The students will gain knowledge of a variety of literary traditions from modern Europe. This will help them to better contextualise English literature of the times.

Suggested Readings:
1. Travers, Martin. An Introduction to Modern European Literature. Macmillan, 1998.
2. Smith, Horatio ed. Columbia Dictionary of Modern European Literature. Columbia University Press, 1947.
3. Foster, Margaret E. A Handbook of Modern European Literature. Wentworth Press, 2016.
