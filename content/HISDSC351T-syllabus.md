HISDSC351T 
HISTORY OF MODERN WORLD FROM 1914 TO 1945 CE 
Contact Hours: 60 
Full Marks = 100 [ESE (70) CCA (30)] 
Course Objective: 
This course aims to provide an understanding of an era of shifting history from Euro-centric to World. It 
discusses the turbulent times when totalitarianism rose as an alternative to democratic and liberal ideals 
and the growing desire for peace through the formation of organizations such as the United Nations.

Expected learning outcomes:

totalitarianism rose as an alternative to democratic and liberal ideals and the growing desire for peace 
through the formation of organizations such as the United Nations.
