DSC-402(B)
Course Title: Advanced Analysis (Option B)
Credits: 4 | Contact Hours: 60 | Marks: 100 [ESE (70) Internal (30)]

Course Objective: The objective of this course is to provide learners with an advanced understanding of mathematical analysis. It covers topics like convergence of functions, power series, functions of bounded variations, and elementary measure theory.

Unit-I:
Pointwise and uniform convergence of sequence and series of functions: definitions, examples and related problems. Cauchy's criterion for uniform convergence and WeierstrassM-Test.

Unit-II:
Limit superior and limit inferior, power series, radius of convergence, Cauchy Hadamard theorem.Applications and related problems.

Unit-III:
Functions of bounded variations: definition, examples, and properties; total variation.

Unit-IV:
O-algebra, Borel sets and Borel o-algebra. Lebesgue outer measure and measurable sets: definition, examples and properties.

Unit-V:
Lebesgue measure, its properties, and the Borel-Cantelli lemma. Cantor set: Construction and topological properties. Recommended Books: 1. R.G. Bartle and D.S. Sherbert, Introduction to Real Analysis, 4th ed.,Wiley, 2014 [Units I-II] 2. T.M. Apostol, Mathematical Analysis, 2nd ed., Narosa, 2002 [Unit II1] 3. H.L. Royden and P.M. Fitzpatrick, Real Analysis, 4th ed., PHI Learning, 2012 [Units IV-V]  ===== PAGE 44 ===== Syllabus of Mathematics DSC Courses

Course Outcome: On completion of the course,learners will be able to analyse and demonstrate convergence properties of functions, determine the convergence of power series, and apply the idea of functions of bounded variations in various contexts. They will also master essential concepts in measure theory, such as o-algebras and Lebesgue measure, and apply them to analyse measurable sets.

Suggested Readings:
1. S. Abbott, Understanding Analysis, 2nd ed., Springer, 2015.
