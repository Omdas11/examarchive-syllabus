DSM-201
Course Title: Classical Algebra and Trigonometry
Credits: 4 | Contact Hours: 60 | Marks: 100

Course Objective:
- The main objective of this course is
- To provide a first approach to the subject of classical algebra.
- To introduce the concept of rank of a matrix, solution of a system of linear equations.
- To familiarize the students with relation between roots and coefficients of a polynomial equation, symmetric function of roots.
- To introduce the concept of inequalities and its use.
- To provide basic knowledge of trigonometry, hyperbolic functions.
- Understanding the summation of trigonometric series, expansions.

Unit-I:
Adjoint of Square matrix, Jacobi's Theorem; Inverse of a square matrix, Elementary transformation on matrices, Rank of a matrix, solution of a system of linear equations by matrix inverse and by Gaussian elimination method.

Unit-II:
Relation between the roots and coefficients of a polynomial equation of nth degree with special reference to cubic equation, Symmetric function of roots; Transformation of equation; Cardan's Method of solution of cubic equation of the form ax*+bx+c=0 (a≠0);

Unit-III:
Inequalities, properties, inequalities involving arithmetic, geometric and harmonic means, Cauchy-Schwarz's inequality, Minkowski inequality, Holder's inequality.  ===== PAGE 70 ===== Syllabus of Mathematics DSM Courses

Unit-IV:
De Moivre's theorem, related problems, Expansion of sin n0 and cos n0, expansion of Sin 0 and Cosθ in ascending powers of 0, functions of complex arguments.

Unit-V:
Gregory's series; summation of trigonometric series; hyperbolic functions.

Suggested Readings:
1. J.G. Chakraborty, PR. Ghosh; Higher Algebra; U.N. Dhur & Sons.
