PHYDSC404P 
LAB: NUMERICAL TECHNIQUES AND VIRTUAL LAB 
Contact Hours: 60 
Full Marks = 100 
Course objective: In this course, the students will learn various aspects of computational techniques and 
train themselves with skills of writing codes for numerical scientific computation and simulations. 
Moreover, this course also will also develop the skills of the students in designing and analyzing 
electrical/electronic circuits through exposure of virtual lab which, will intern enable the students to 
achieve the knowledge of working with various electronic devices and circuits without physically visiting 
the laboratory. 
Two Experiments are to be performed - one from each part
