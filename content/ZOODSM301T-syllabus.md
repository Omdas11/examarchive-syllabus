DSM-301
Course Title: Genetics and Molecular Biology
Credits: 3 | Contact Hours: 45 | Marks: 100

Course Objective: To understand the fundamental principles of genetics and molecular biology

Unit-I: Mendelism and its Extension
1. Principles of inheritance
2. Incomplete dominance and co-dominance
3. Allele concept - multiple alleles, pseudo allele and lethal allele
4. Epistasis, hypostasis and pleiotrophy

Unit-II: Linkage, Crossing Over and Mutation
1. Linkage - types of linkage, linkage groups and significance
2. Crossing over - types of crossing over, mechanism and significance
3. Mutation - Gene and chromosome mutations

Unit-III: Sex Determination, Sex-linked Inheritance and Extra-chromosomal Inheritance
1. Chromosomal, cytoplasmic, hormonal and environmental basis of sex determination
2. Sex-linked inheritance
3. Extra-chromosomal inheritance

Unit-IV: Nucleic Acids
1. Salient features and chemical composition of DNA and RNA
2. Watson and Crick model of DNA; Types and functions of DNA
3. Structure of RNA - its types and functions

Unit-V: DNA Replication, Transcription and Translation
1. DNA replication - types and mechanism of DNA replication
2. RNA polymerases, mechanism of transcription if Prokaryotes
3. Genetic code, mechanism of translation in Prokaryotes

Course Outcome: Students will able to demonstrate a comprehensive understanding of genetics and molecular biology, including the ability to analyze genetic data.

Suggested Readings:
1. Gardner, E.J., Simmons, M.J., Snustad, D.P. (2008). Principles of Genetics. VIII Edition. Wiley
2. Griffiths, A.J.F., Wessler, S.R., Lewontin, R.C. and Carroll, S.B. Introduction to Genetic Analysis. IX Edition. W. H. Freeman and Co
3. Strickberger MW (2015). Genetics. III Edition. Pearson Education India
4. Karp, G. (2010) Cell and Molecular Biology: Concepts and Experiments. VI Edition. John Wiley
5. Kleinsmith, L.J., Hardin. J. and Bertoni, G. P. (2009). The World of the Cell. VII Edition. Pearson Benjamin Cummings Publishing, San Francisco.
6. Bruce Alberts, Alexander Johnson, Julian Lewis, Martin Raff, Keith Roberts, Peter Walter: Molecular Biology of the Cell, IV Edition.
