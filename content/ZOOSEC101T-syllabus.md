SEC-101
Course Title: Apiculture
Credits: 3 | Contact Hours: 60 | Marks: 100

Course Objective: To inculcate skills in apiary management

Unit-I: Biology of Bees
1. History, Classification and Biology of Honey Bees
2. Social Organization of Bee Colony

Unit-II: Rearing of Bees
1. Artificial Bee rearing (Apiary), Beehives - Newton and Langstroth
2. Bee Pasturage, Selection of Bee Species for Apiculture
3. Bee Keeping Equipment
4. Methods of Extraction of Honey (Indigenous and Modern)

Unit-III: Diseases and Enemies
1. Bee Diseases and Enemies
2. Control and Preventive measures

Unit-IV: Bee Economy
1. Products of Apiculture Industry and its Uses (Honey, Bees Wax, Propolis), Pollen

Unit-V: Entrepreneurship in Apiculture
1. Bee Keeping Industry - Govt initiatives and recent efforts
2. Modern methods in employing artificial Beehives for cross pollination in horticultural gardens

Course Outcome: Students will be able to apply their knowledge and skills in apiculture to establish and manage their own beekeeping operations successfully.
