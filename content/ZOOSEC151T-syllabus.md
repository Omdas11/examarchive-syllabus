SEC-151
Course Title: Medical Diagnostics
Credits: 3 | Contact Hours: 60 | Marks: 100

Course Objective: To understand the principles and techniques used in medical diagnostics

Unit-I: Introduction and Haematology
1. Importance of medical diagnostics
2. Blood composition, Preparation of blood smear and Differential Leucocyte Count (DLC) using Leishman's stain
3. Platelet count using haemocytometer
4. Erythrocyte Sedimentary Rate (ESR), Packed Cell Volume (PCV)

Unit-II: Diagnostic Methods used for Sputum, Urine and Stool Analysis
1. Sputum analysis: Physical characteristics and abnormal constituents
2. Urine Analysis: Physical characteristics and abnormal constituents
3. Stool Analysis: Physical characteristics and abnormal constituents

Unit-III: Non-infectious Diseases
1. Diabetes (Type I and Type II): Causes, types, symptoms, complications, diagnosis and prevention
2. Hypertension (Primary and secondary): Causes, types, symptoms, complications, diagnosis and prevention
3. Cancer: Basics, benign and malignant tumour, metastasis, Detection

Unit-IV: Infectious Diseases
1. Tuberculosis: Causes, types, symptoms, diagnosis and prevention
2. Hepatitis: Causes, types, symptoms, diagnosis and prevention

Unit-V: Medical Imaging Techniques
1. X-Ray: Basic principle and applications
2. CT Scan: Basic principle, types and applications
3. MRI: Basic principle, types and applications
4. Sonography: Basic principle and applications

Course Outcome: Students will be able to apply knowledge of medical diagnostic techniques for disease identification and management.
